# Tick-shock Step 15K: clean excursion evidence

Step 15K replaced ambiguous horizon overshoot with explicit target/quote/lag evidence and a 30-second censor boundary, then measured ATR-normalized clean excursions without changing the detector or adding orders.

The March development sample contains a clean-move subset, and the preregistered high-movement state is associated with a materially higher clean-move rate. The effect is concentrated in USDJPY and has not been tested OOS, so no strategy parameter or production promotion is justified. Full evidence and limitations are in [the Step 15K result](../research/tick_shock/15k_tradeable_move_cross_symbol_results.md) and `reports/analysis/tick_shock/step15k/`.
